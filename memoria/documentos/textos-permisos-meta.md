Antes de enviar: completa el test "Conecta con los clientes a través de WhatsApp" (sección Tests), con la cuenta de WhatsApp Business de Kaizen Consulting.

## 1. whatsapp_business_messaging

**Uso (inglés, para Meta):**
Zentralia WB is a customer-messaging panel for businesses. The app receives messages that customers send to a business's WhatsApp Business number through the WhatsApp Cloud API, shows them in the business's inbox, and sends replies (text and approved template messages) on behalf of the business. An AI assistant drafts replies from the business's own knowledge base; a human agent can review or take over at any time.

**Pasos para el revisor (inglés):**
1. Log in to the Zentralia panel with the test account provided in the review notes.
2. Open "Redes sociales" and select the WhatsApp channel.
3. Send a message to the test WhatsApp number from a personal phone.
4. The message appears in the inbox within seconds.
5. Reply from the panel. The reply is delivered to the phone.

**Qué grabar en el video:** pasos 2 a 5 en pantalla, con el teléfono visible o con el mensaje entrante y la respuesta en el chat.

## 2. whatsapp_business_management

**Uso (inglés):**
The app reads the WhatsApp Business Accounts, phone numbers and message templates that belong to the business, so the business can see which numbers are connected, check their status, and create or submit message templates (for example order confirmations and payment reminders) for Meta approval.

**Pasos para el revisor (inglés):**
1. Log in to the Zentralia panel with the test account.
2. Open the WhatsApp connection screen.
3. The panel lists the connected WhatsApp Business Account and its phone numbers.
4. Open the templates list; it shows each template and its approval status.

**Qué grabar en el video:** la pantalla de cuentas y números conectados, y la lista de plantillas con su estado.

## 3. business_management

**Uso (inglés):**
Businesses use Zentralia to manage the Meta assets they own (Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business Account). The app reads the assets assigned to the business in its Business Manager so the business can confirm which pages and accounts are connected to Zentralia. It does not change the business's assets without the business's action.

**Pasos para el revisor (inglés):**
1. Log in to the Zentralia panel with the test account.
2. Open the connected-channels screen.
3. The panel lists the business's Facebook Page, Instagram account and WhatsApp Business Account, as shared through Business Manager.

**Qué grabar en el video:** la pantalla de canales conectados con esas cuentas.

---

## 4. public_profile

**Uso (inglés):**
When a user signs in with Facebook, the app reads the user's public profile (name and ID) to identify the person in the Zentralia panel and to show who is signed in. No other profile data is requested.

**Pasos para el revisor (inglés):**
1. Open the Zentralia panel login page.
2. Click "Log in with Facebook".
3. Approve the request. The panel shows the user's name in the top bar.

**Qué grabar en el video:** el botón de inicio de sesión, la ventana de Facebook con el permiso y la pantalla con el nombre del usuario.
